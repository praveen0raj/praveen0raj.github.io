About Me

I'm a Principal Architect based in Bengaluru, India, with a track record of delivering production systems from zero-to-one in startup and scale-up environments. I specialize in payments, enterprise SaaS, and multi-tenant platforms.

My approach centers on trade-off-driven architecture decisions — evaluating options like Auth0 vs Keycloak for multi-tenant SSO, PostgreSQL RLS vs application-level tenant isolation, or event-driven vs synchronous processing, and choosing what best fits the context.

Beyond code, I focus on scaling engineering teams, establishing architecture review processes, and creating design doc standards that elevate the entire organization.
